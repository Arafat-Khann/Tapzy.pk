About Tapzy

We're making everyday interactions simpler, one tap at a time.

We are a Pakistan-based NFC and QR solutions company focused on turning everyday tasks into simple, tap-to-connect experiences.

We design and build custom NFC and QR products from scratch, tailored to how businesses and individuals actually use them. From networking and social profiles to menus, reviews, and other everyday interactions, our products are designed to make sharing information faster, easier, and more convenient.

We don't just sell pre-made cards. We build solutions around the way you need to use them. Every product can be customized to your requirements and is available for both individual orders and bulk business needs.

Our Mission

We believe NFC should be more than a feature on your phone. It should be a practical part of everyday life.

Our mission is to make NFC accessible, efficient, and useful across Pakistan, replacing unnecessary steps, repetitive tasks, and physical exchanges with something as simple as a tap.

We want to bring more of everyday life into one simple interaction:

Tap. Connect. Done.
