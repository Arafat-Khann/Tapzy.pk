How it works

From counter to review in three steps

01

Place it where customers finish

Keep the card near your cashier, reception desk, checkout or table where a positive interaction has just happened.

02

They tap or scan

A customer taps an NFC-enabled phone on the card, or opens their camera and scans the printed QR code.

03

Your Google review page opens

The configured link takes them straight to your review page. A happy customer can share feedback without typing or searching.

Do I need to design anything?

No. Send your Google review link after ordering and Tapzy pre-programs the card before dispatch.

What if a customer's phone does not support NFC?

They can scan the printed QR code with their phone camera.

Can I change the link later?

Yes. The NFC chip can be reprogrammed. Contact Tapzy for help.
