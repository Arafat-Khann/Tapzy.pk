# SEO upgrade report

## Scope and branch

Implemented on the `seo-upgrade` branch only. Nothing was pushed, merged, or deployed. The requested static product/category pages are generated by `scripts/generate-pages.js`, which reads the product data in `js/products.js` so prices and specifications stay aligned.

## What changed

- Replaced visible `Tapzy.pk` branding with `Tapzy`; the LinkedIn copy and source note use `@tapzypk` as requested.
- Added static, JavaScript-independent page content for the homepage, Google review cards, digital menu cards, NFC business cards, Instagram and LinkedIn cards, custom NFC cards, and bulk/reseller enquiries.
- Kept cart, checkout, quantity pricing, Buy Now, and WhatsApp flows on the existing JavaScript implementation.
- Added canonical/OG/Twitter metadata, `en_PK` locale, favicon variants, Organization/WebSite/Product JSON-LD, breadcrumbs, FAQs, internal links, and a persistent WhatsApp CTA.
- Added `robots.txt`, a hand-maintainable `sitemap.xml`, `vercel.json`, a real 404 page, and the exact Search Console verification file.
- Preserved source images and notes in `_originals/`; raw source content is excluded from Vercel. Removed unused duplicate root/logo files after confirming no code references them.
- Added optimized product WebP/JPG variants, a compact logo, favicon sizes, and a social-share image. Existing fallback JPG names remain stable for the commerce code.

## Deliberate non-changes

- No city pages were created; they would repeat content and risk thin pages.
- No fake reviews, ratings, stock/availability, address, payment claim, return policy, client name, case study, or business hours were added.
- No `availability` or aggregate rating schema was added.
- Existing coded prices were not changed. The Google review pricing jump from PKR 2,500 for 1–49 to PKR 600 for 50–99 is surfaced for owner confirmation.
- The old `product.html?id=` template remains as a compatibility fallback, while Vercel redirects known IDs to static URLs.
